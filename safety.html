What our certifications actually mean

The checks, in plain English

No jargon, just what we do and why it matters for your family's safety.

Association

BIHA Membership

We're members of the British Inflatable Hirers Alliance. Membership means our equipment, insurance, and operating standards are checked against the industry's official code of practice, not just our own word for it.

Electrical safety

PAT Tested

Portable Appliance Testing on every blower unit, every year. It confirms the electrics driving your castle are safe to plug in and run for the full length of your party.

Inflatable safety mark

PIPA Tested

Every castle carries a PIPA tag, an annual structural inspection by an independent examiner covering seams, anchor points, and fall height. If a unit doesn't pass, it doesn't leave the yard.

Cover

Public Liability Insurance

We carry £5 million public liability insurance on every booking, so you and your guests are covered for the day. A copy of our certificate is available on request.

Hygiene

Cleaned Before Every Hire

Every castle is wiped down, disinfected, and inspected before it goes back out, not just when it looks dirty. What arrives at your garden has been cleaned specifically for your booking.

On the day

Full Delivery Service

We deliver, anchor down correctly for your ground type, run a safety check with you, and collect at the end. You never have to inflate, secure, or pack away anything yourself.

Before you book

What we need from you, and what happens on the day

Space & ground

A flat, reasonably level area (grass, patio, or hard standing) with enough clearance around the castle's footprint. We'll ask for rough dimensions when you enquire so we can match the right size to your space.

Power

A standard household socket within reach of an extension lead does the job. If you don't have outdoor power, let us know and we'll talk through options.

Adult supervision

A responsible adult must supervise the castle at all times while it's in use, keeping numbers and age groups sensible and stepping in if things get too rough.

Shoes off, no sharp items

No shoes, glasses, jewellery, face paint, or food and drink on the castle. It keeps the material in good condition and stops anyone getting hurt on the bounce.

Weather policy

Inflatables can't safely be used in strong wind or heavy rain. If the forecast turns on the day, we'll be in touch to talk through rescheduling, no awkward conversations needed.

Delivery & collection

We arrive ahead of your start time, set up, secure the castle, and run through the safety points with you. We collect at an agreed time once the fun's over.
